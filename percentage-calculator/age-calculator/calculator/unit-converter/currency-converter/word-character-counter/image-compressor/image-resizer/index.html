What is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the width and height of an image. It can be useful when an image needs specific dimensions for websites, social media, documents or other applications.

How to Resize an Image

Select an image, enter your desired width and height, choose whether to keep the original aspect ratio, and click Resize Image.

Privacy

Images are processed directly in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which image formats are supported?
JPG, PNG and WebP images are supported.

Can I keep the original aspect ratio?
Yes. Keep aspect ratio is enabled by default.
