What is a PDF Compressor?

A PDF compressor is a tool that helps reduce the size of PDF documents. Smaller PDF files are easier to store, upload and share.

How to Compress a PDF

Select a PDF file, choose a compression level and click Compress PDF.

Privacy

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF Compressor free?

Can every PDF become smaller?
Not necessarily. Some PDFs are already highly compressed. In those cases, reducing the file further may not be possible without changing the document.
