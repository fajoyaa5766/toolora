What is PDF to Word?

PDF to Word conversion changes text from a PDF document into an editable Word document.

How to Convert PDF to Word

Select a PDF file and click Convert to Word. The extracted text will be placed into a Word-compatible document.

Important Information

This version works best with PDFs containing selectable text. Scanned PDFs and complex layouts may require OCR and advanced document processing.

Privacy

The conversion is performed in your browser. Your PDF does not need to be uploaded to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert scanned PDFs?
Scanned PDFs usually require OCR. This initial version focuses on text-based PDFs.

Is this tool free?
Yes. Toolora's PDF to Word tool is free to use.
