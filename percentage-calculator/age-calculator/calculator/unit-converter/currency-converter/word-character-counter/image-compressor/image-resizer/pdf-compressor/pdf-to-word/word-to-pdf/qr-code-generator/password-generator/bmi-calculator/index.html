What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a number calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening measure.

BMI Categories for Adults

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or higher Obesity

How to Calculate BMI

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?
BMI is a general screening measure and may not accurately represent body composition for every person.
