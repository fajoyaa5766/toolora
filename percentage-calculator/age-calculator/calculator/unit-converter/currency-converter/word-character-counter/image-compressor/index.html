What is an Image Compressor?

An image compressor reduces the file size of an image while trying to maintain good visual quality. Smaller images can load faster on websites and use less storage.

How to Compress an Image

Select your image, choose the compression quality and click the Compress Image button. Then download the compressed file.

Privacy

Your selected image is processed directly in your browser. The tool does not need to upload your image to a server.
