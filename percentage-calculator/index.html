What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator is a simple online tool that helps you calculate a percentage of a number quickly.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100.

Example:
20% of 150 = (20 × 150) ÷ 100 = 30
